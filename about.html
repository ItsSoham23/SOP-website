About

The Environmental Futures Archive is a space for students to imagine the environmental futures they may inherit and help shape. Through short stories, the archive holds open a question that is both speculative and immediate: how might people, other species, technology and place change over the next century?

Each contribution responds to the prompt, “What does the environment look like in 100 years?” The stories are not predictions. They are invitations to notice the choices we are making now, to imagine alternatives, and to consider what care, adaptation and responsibility might look like in the years ahead.
