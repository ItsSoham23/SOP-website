Share your environmental future

Prompt: “What does the environment look like in 100 years?”

We invite undergraduate students pursuing any degree to submit original stories of less than 1000 words that respond to the prompt above. Write from your heart and your visions for the future.

Before you submit

  • Write an original story of fewer than 1000 words.
  • Do not use AI tools to generate the text.
  • Stories may be lightly copy-edited for spelling, punctuation and clarity, without changing your meaning.
  • We may decline a story, and may remove a published story if there is a reason to.
  • You keep the copyright to your story.
