About

The Environmental Futures Archive is an educational initiative that documents young people’s imagination of the future. What will the world look like? Will we be here? There is so much environmental awareness targeting the youth and an urgent need felt to bestow them with environmental knowledge. The archive simply collects stories by students as ways of thinking about the future, as ways of working through the present.

Students write in response to a single prompt: “What does the environment look like in 100 years?”
