Environmental Futures Archive

How young people imagine life in a changing world

The Environmental Futures Archive is a collection of stories by students who are imagining possible environmental futures. This project follows how young people think about the environment and how it is going to change in the future, and all the anticipated transformations—of animal life, technology, myths. Students respond to a single prompt: “What will the environment look like in 100 years?”
